# A.D — Design direction

## Visual character

Quiet, deliberate creative-finance workspace: editorial in its typography, exact in its evidence, and direct in its actions. Black and white are the only brand colors. Use neutral grays for hierarchy, dividers, and disabled states. No blue, purple, green, or decorative gradients. Data may use labels, line style, and shape as well as neutral tones; never depend on color alone.

Use Bodoni Moda sparingly for a strong editorial title and Inter for compact controls, evidence, and readable body text. Do not turn every heading into a hero. Keep product identity distinct from a generic finance dashboard.

## Hierarchy and composition

- Four destinations only: Aujourd’hui, Radar, Travail, Bibliothèque.
- Every screen states where the user is, what the information means, and the next useful action.
- One primary action per screen. Secondary actions remain visually quiet.
- Prefer a clear page grid, rows, timelines, and open canvas over containers inside containers. Never place nested cards around every section.
- Separate facts, sources, interpretation, unknowns, AI draft, and human decision through labels and layout, not colored decoration.
- Public A.D Studio and the future multi-user product get separate entry points and distinct onboarding.
- Keep legal, infrastructure, account-recovery, connection diagnostics, and sync controls out of everyday workflows; expose them only where relevant.

## Mobile is a primary surface

- Design and verify at 390×844 and a compact 390×640 before desktop polish.
- Use a stable four-item bottom navigation with safe-area padding, 44px minimum touch targets, and no overlap with the current action.
- Keep page titles readable, content in one column, evidence links tappable, and actions reachable without horizontal scrolling.
- Use 16px minimum text in inputs, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, and explicit loading/empty/error/offline states.
- Capture, search, and return from a source without losing the current opportunity or dossier.

## Content and behavior rules

- UI copy is concise French and names the actual state. Avoid mixed-language labels and generic status jargon.
- A source is visible near the claim it supports. A publication date is never presented as an event date.
- A generated direction, offer, or contract is always an editable draft and shows its source context and unknowns.
- Never show a disconnected empty module as a large panel in the daily workspace.
- No fake photos, example opportunities, decorative metrics, social proof, or success state.
- The first useful action should be obvious within five seconds; every step must lead to the next without requiring the user to remember a hidden relationship.

## Reference use

Use Impeccable’s product-truth/design-direction separation and its warnings against generic SaaS layouts, card nesting, and decorative complexity. Use Revolut only for legibility and controlled hierarchy. Use Ophelia as an inspiration for working with references and creating, not as a copied visual system. A.D’s own black-and-white editorial workspace remains the identity.
